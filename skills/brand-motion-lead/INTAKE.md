# Intake: ask, then execute

The lead always starts a piece of work with a short interview, then builds without further check-ins
except the stills gate. Ask everything in **one message**, numbered, with a sensible default in
brackets for each so the person can answer "defaults" and be done. Never ask more than eight.

## First run (BRAND.md is still the blank template)

1. What's the brand and its site? [I'll pull the logo, colours and fonts from the site]
2. In one line, what does it do, and for whom?
3. Three words for how it should feel. [calm, precise, warm]
4. Anything you never want to see? [gradients, glows, stock 3D, sparkles]
5. Who's in your cast for demos? Names, roles, tools. [I'll make a small labelled composite team]

Fill BRAND.md from the answers and the live site, show the filled file in five lines, then go on to
the task.

## Every new piece

Ask only what the brief doesn't already say:

1. What are we making? [a 20–30 s product film]
2. Who's it for, and what should they be able to say after watching?
3. Formats and length. [16:9 master + 9:16 + 1:1, 20 s]
4. The one product moment to show, with real screens if you have them. [I'll capture your live site]
5. Voice: your recording, a synthetic voice, or captions only? [synthetic narration + captions]
6. Music: a licensed track (with a clear beat), or should I synthesize one? [synthesize]
7. Anything that must or must not appear? (logos, claims, names, numbers with sources)
8. Do you want to approve the beat map and stills before the full render? [no: I'll show them and keep going]

## Then execute

- Make the calls the answers leave open; log each in one line in `DECISIONS.md`.
- Show the beat map and one still per beat, then build, render, run `kit/checks.sh` and the critique
  pass, and deliver the finished files with a short note of what was tested.
- Stop again only for missing rights (music, logos, people's likeness), unsafe content, or an
  ambiguity that changes the goal.
