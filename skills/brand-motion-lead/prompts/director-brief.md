# Director's brief (long or flagship films)

Fill every heading before any code. A brief with a blank heading is not ready.

1. **Logline:** one sentence: who, what changes, why it matters.
2. **Feeling and audience:** the one emotion, who must feel it, and what they believe before watching.
3. **The one question:** what the film answers, in the viewer's words; the must-know facts and the cut list.
4. **References:** two or three named styles or films. Say what to take (palette, pacing, camera, transitions) and what not to take (content).
5. **Directions:** three or four looks side by side in one `directions.html`; mark the winner and why.
6. **Engine and format:** HyperFrames or the seek(t) kit; fps; 16:9 master plus native 9:16 and 1:1; duration.
7. **Brand:** point at BRAND.md; list any departures and why.
8. **Cast:** from the cast bible; label composites.
9. **Scenes:** one row each: time, what it teaches, what is on screen in order of importance, the motion (enter, key action, settle, exit), words, the sound cue, and what the viewer can now say.
10. **Text on screen:** two lines at most, about eight words a line, minimum size readable at 360 px.
11. **Sound:** score (supplied or synthesized), cues only on actions that mean something, narration fitted to scenes, music ducked under voice, loudness target.
12. **Gates:** plan → stills → animatic at half size → full render → polish → audio → final. Show stills and the animatic; keep building unless asked to wait. Log calls made on a thin brief in `DECISIONS.md`.
13. **Deliverables:** films in every format, captions (`.srt`), poster frame, contact sheet, source, and a README that lists only what was tested.
